The embedding model performs the semantic representation. The optional learner is a tiny local calibrator trained only on your own Correct / Incorrect marks (activates after 20 labeled examples, blended at a limited weight). It has not been trained on recruiters, ATS decisions, or hiring outcomes.

Ranked Resumes

The Match Score is an internal comparative estimate of textual, semantic, functional, and evidence alignment. It is not an employer ATS score, does not reproduce any specific employer's screening system, and does not predict interview or hiring likelihood.
